# Security Planner Handoff

## Decision Context

* **ADR ID:** ADR-0001
* **ADR:** docs/planning/adrs/0001-choose-agentic-hr-architecture.md
* **Status:** proposed
* **Compact summary:** adr-0001-compact-summary.md

## STRIDE-Relevant Excerpt

Re-examine the trust boundaries created by Teams and Azure Bot Service, Entra ID OBO, the App Service agent and internal MCP server, Azure AI Foundry and AI Search, the two Cosmos DB stores, Service Bus/Functions, and outbound Workday/SAP and Microsoft Graph connectors.

* **Identity and authorization:** Validate delegated identity and token audience across Teams, OBO, MCP, Graph, and HRIS connectors. Define least-privilege scopes and enforce tenant isolation, employee self-access, manager direct-report scope, and HR-admin permissions at the data and tool boundaries.
* **Sensitive data:** Treat medical reasons and compensation values as restricted. Minimize fields sent to models, search indexes, conversation memory, Teams cards, telemetry, and logs. Verify that managers see only sanitized leave details and cannot access diagnosis or unrelated compensation data.
* **MCP and integration boundary:** Review tool allowlisting, caller authorization, input validation, outbound destinations, credential storage, secret rotation, and failure behavior. Ensure the agent cannot use a tool beyond the requesting user's authority.
* **Audit integrity:** The ADR notes that Cosmos DB does not itself establish immutable audit records. Define append-only or tamper-evident controls, privileged access monitoring, retention, and evidence export.
* **Threat scenarios:** Assess spoofed or replayed approval actions, ticket/identity tampering, repudiation, sensitive-data disclosure through prompts or cards, denial of service through queue or model dependencies, and privilege escalation through connector credentials.

## Security Planner Focus

Map the data flows and trust boundaries, identify STRIDE threats and controls, and produce negative tests for cross-tenant access, unauthorized manager scope, approval replay, prompt/data leakage, and connector misuse. The source evidence is synthetic workshop material, and applicable legal or regulatory obligations remain unverified.

## RAI-Relevant Excerpt

The proposal selects Azure AI Foundry for model hosting and moderation, with hybrid BM25/vector Azure AI Search and semantic reranking for policy retrieval. The agent provides conversational policy answers and ticket-workflow assistance. Managers and HR administrators retain decision authority; the agent must not approve, reject, or infer medical diagnoses.

## Trustworthiness Characteristics to Assess

* **Valid and reliable:** Measure retrieval relevance, citation correctness, answer completeness, abstention when policy evidence is missing or conflicting, and robustness to stale or malformed policy documents.
* **Safe and privacy-enhanced:** Test PHI minimization, medical-diagnosis suppression in manager views, compensation confidentiality, prompt-injection resistance in retrieved content, and leakage through model context, memory, telemetry, and audit records.
* **Secure and resilient:** Assess tool authorization, model/retrieval dependency failures, moderation behavior, safe fallback, and protection against unauthorized actions.
* **Accountable and transparent:** Preserve source citations, distinguish policy text from generated explanation, record attributable lifecycle actions, and make the human approval boundary visible.
* **Fair and equitable:** Check whether policy answers and workflow handling remain consistent across worker types, leave categories, and authorized access roles; route ambiguous or exceptional cases to HR rather than inferring eligibility.

## Model Lifecycle and Stakeholders

* **Govern and Map:** Identify HR policy owners, employees, line managers, HR administrators, security/privacy, and the accountable model owner. Confirm intended use, prohibited medical inference, data classes, escalation ownership, and jurisdictional constraints.
* **Measure:** Establish pre-release and regression evaluations for grounded answers, citation fidelity, refusal/abstention, privacy leakage, moderation, role-specific output, and prompt injection. Keep the synthetic workshop evidence separate from verified production policy.
* **Manage:** Monitor grounded-answer quality, privacy/security incidents, fallback and escalation rates, retrieval/index changes, model and prompt versions, and approved-override outcomes. Define rollback and incident response before production use.
* **Human impact:** Employees rely on policy explanations and ticket status; managers receive sanitized approval context; HR handles exceptions and escalations. No model output substitutes for a manager or HR decision.

## Open Inputs

Confirm the production policy corpus and owner, approved model/deployment and retention settings, evaluation thresholds, and how the conflicting 48-hour versus 72-hour SLA evidence will be resolved. Treat the ADR alternatives and rejection rationales as user-reported, not independently verified.
